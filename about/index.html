About

SEGEN Healthcare Recruitment

SEGEN connects qualified nurses from India with hospitals, nursing homes, and other healthcare facilities in Germany. We support recruitment, selection, professional recognition, visa processing, documentation, coordination, and entry into Germany.

Team

An Indian-German team

Our Indian-German team covers both sides of the process. That means candidates and employers work with people who understand the requirements in India and the expectations of healthcare facilities in Germany.

Language academy

Own German Language Academy in India

Language preparation sits alongside nursing qualifications at our own academy in India. We cooperate with a telc examination centre in India. After B2-level training, candidates can get timely exam appointments subject to examination-centre availability.
