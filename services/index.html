What we support

Eight services

Nurse Recruitment

Identifying and recruiting qualified, motivated nurses in India for roles with German healthcare providers.

Hospital & Nursing Home Placement

Connecting candidates with hospitals, nursing homes, and other healthcare facilities in Germany.

Candidate Selection & Coordination

Supporting selection and coordinating between candidates and employers throughout the process.

German Language Preparation

Language training through SEGEN’s own German Language Academy in India, combining nursing qualifications with German language preparation. telc exam appointments after B2-level training are subject to examination-centre availability.

Professional Recognition Process

Support with the professional recognition process required to work as a nurse in Germany.

Visa Processing Support

Assistance with visa processing as part of the journey from India to Germany.

Documentation & Coordination

Support for documentation and coordination across both countries.

Personal Support

Personal support for candidates and employers, including coordination around entry into Germany.
